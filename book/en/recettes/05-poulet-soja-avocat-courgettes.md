## Ingredients
- 2 chicken or turkey breasts
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 garlic clove, 1 small piece of ginger (optional)
- 2 courgettes
- 1 avocado (½ per person)
- 3 spring onions
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Sesame seeds, lime
- Brown rice or buckwheat noodles (optional)

## Method
1. Cut the poultry into strips. Mix with the soy sauce, sesame oil, and grated garlic and ginger. Leave to marinate while you prepare the rest.
2. Cut the courgettes into half-moons and slice the spring onions.
3. In a hot pan with the olive oil, sauté the courgettes for 5 minutes. Set aside.
4. In the same pan, cook the poultry for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until cooked through.
5. Return the courgettes, add the spring onions and stir for a minute.
6. Serve with sliced avocado, sesame seeds and a squeeze of lime.

## Variation
Plant-based version: replace the poultry with firm tofu, marinated the same way.
