## Ingredients
- 2 fillets of sea bream, sea bass or another medium-sized fish (or 1 whole gutted fish)
- 1 large fennel bulb
- 1 lemon
- 1 handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Dill, parsley or fennel fronds
- Salt, pepper
- Quinoa, rice or potatoes to serve

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Cut the fennel into thin wedges and the lemon into rounds. Spread them on a tray with the olives, drizzle with oil, season with salt and pepper.
3. Roast for 18 to 20 minutes, until the fennel starts to brown.
4. Lay the fillets on the vegetables, skin side down, with a drizzle of oil. A whole fish needs about 20 to 25 minutes depending on its size.
5. Return to the oven for 10 to 12 minutes for fillets: the flesh should be opaque and flake easily.
6. Sprinkle with herbs and serve with your starch.

## The right fish
Choose wild, locally caught fish when possible: ask your fishmonger what has just come in. Start cooking the quinoa at the same time as the fennel, and everything will be ready together.
