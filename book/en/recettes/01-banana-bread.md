## Ingredients
- 3 very ripe bananas (speckled)
- 2 eggs
- 60 g maple syrup or unrefined sugar
- 60 ml mild olive oil or rapeseed oil
- 125 g plain yoghurt (or plant-based)
- 200 g spelt flour or light wholemeal wheat flour
- 1 tbsp blond psyllium
- 1 sachet baking powder (about 2 tsp)
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 pinch of salt
- 80 g dark chocolate chips

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Oil and flour a loaf tin, or line it with baking paper.
2. Mash the bananas with a fork. Add the eggs, syrup, oil and yoghurt, then whisk.
3. Stir in the psyllium and leave for 5 minutes: it will thicken the batter.
4. Add the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt. Mix just until there's no dry flour left.
5. Add the chocolate chips (keep a few for the top), pour into the tin.
6. Bake for 45 to 55 minutes: the tip of a knife should come out almost dry. Let it cool a little before turning out.

## My tip
Psyllium replaces butter for softness: it holds water and binds the batter. A slice with a little hazelnut butter is my D24 snack.
