## Ingredients
- 150 g fromage blanc (or Greek yoghurt, or a plant-based alternative)
- 1 orange
- 2 tbsp toasted buckwheat (kasha)
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 pinch of flaky salt (optional)
- 1 or 2 eggs
- A few mint or basil leaves (optional)

## Method
1. Soft-boiled eggs: lower them into boiling water for 6 minutes, then into cold water, and peel. Fried eggs: 3 to 4 minutes in an oiled pan, covered.
2. Peel the orange, removing the pith, and cut it into rounds or segments. Keep the juice that runs out.
3. In a bowl, add the fromage blanc, then the orange and its juice.
4. Sprinkle with toasted buckwheat, drizzle with olive oil, add the flaky salt and herbs.
5. Serve with the eggs on the side.

## Variation
Out of citrus season: pan-fried pear or apple, and a pinch of cinnamon.
