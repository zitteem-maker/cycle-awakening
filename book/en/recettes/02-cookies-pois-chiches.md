## Ingredients
- 1 tin of chickpeas (about 240 g drained)
- 60 g peanut or almond butter
- 60 g maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 60 g rolled oats
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 pinch of salt
- 40 g chopped walnuts
- 60 g frozen red berries (not thawed) or dried cranberries

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Rinse the chickpeas and dry them well in a tea towel.
2. Blend the chickpeas with the nut butter, syrup and vanilla until smooth.
3. Add the oats, baking powder and salt, and blend for a few seconds.
4. Stir in the walnuts and berries with a spoon.
5. Shape 12 balls and flatten them on a tray lined with baking paper.
6. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until the edges turn golden. Let them cool on the tray: they firm up as they cool.

## The brave option
Prefer to start from dried chickpeas? Soak them overnight, then simmer them for 1 to 1½ hours (or about 30 minutes in a pressure cooker) before using. Dried chickpeas should never be eaten raw or undercooked.
