## Ingredients
- **Golden milk:** 250 ml milk or plant-based drink (oat, almond), ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cinnamon, 1 pinch of ground ginger, 1 pinch of pepper, 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
- **Chai latte:** 1 tbsp loose chai (I use Prana Chai), 100 ml water, 150 ml milk or plant-based drink

## Method
1. **Golden milk:** heat the milk with the spices without boiling, whisking. Off the heat, add the honey or syrup. Whisk to froth.
2. **Chai latte:** bring the water to a simmer with the chai and leave to infuse for 3 to 5 minutes.
3. Add the milk and heat gently without boiling.
4. Strain into the mug. Froth if you like.

## My tip
A hot mug at the same time every evening becomes a signal: the day is over.
