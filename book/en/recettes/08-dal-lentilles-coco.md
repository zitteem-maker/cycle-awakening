## Ingredients
- 200 g red lentils
- 1 onion, 1 garlic clove
- 1 piece of fresh ginger
- 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp olive or coconut oil
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 400 ml water or vegetable stock
- 2 handfuls of baby spinach
- 1 lime, fresh coriander
- Basmati rice or naan bread to serve

## Method
1. Rinse the lentils until the water runs clear.
2. Soften the sliced onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, grated ginger and spices, for one minute.
3. Add the lentils, coconut milk and water. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft.
4. Off the heat, add the spinach: it wilts in a minute. Season with salt.
5. Serve with a squeeze of lime, coriander and rice.

## My tip
It freezes very well in individual portions: a real rescue meal for D26.
