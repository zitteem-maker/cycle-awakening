## Ingredients
- 6 soft dates (Medjool if possible)
- 2 tbsp hazelnut butter
- 1 tbsp toasted buckwheat
- 1 pinch of flaky salt
- 20 g dark chocolate (optional)

## Method
1. Slit the dates lengthways and remove the stone.
2. Fill each one with a small spoonful of hazelnut butter.
3. Sprinkle with toasted buckwheat and a grain of flaky salt.
4. Optional: melt the chocolate and drizzle it over each date. Leave to set for 10 minutes in the fridge.

## My tip
Make a box on Sunday: they keep for four days in the fridge. It's my 4 pm snack at the end of my cycle.
