## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp dried lemon verbena (or 1 small handful fresh)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- The zest of ½ unwaxed orange
- 500 ml water
- 1 tsp honey (optional)

## Method
1. Bring the water to a simmer (not a rolling boil).
2. Put the verbena, cinnamon and zest in a teapot.
3. Pour in the hot water, cover and leave to infuse for 7 to 10 minutes.
4. Strain, and add the honey if you like.

## My ritual
I make it while I tidy the kitchen, and I drink it away from screens. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or on treatment, ask for advice before regularly drinking a herbal infusion.
