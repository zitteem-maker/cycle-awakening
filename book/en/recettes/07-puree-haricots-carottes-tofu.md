## Ingredients
- 1 jar of white beans (about 400 g)
- 1 garlic clove, the juice of ½ lemon
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 carrots, 1 tsp cumin
- 200 g firm tofu, or 2 eggs (see the variation)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp maple syrup (or honey, if you're not looking for a vegan version)
- 1 handful of green vegetables (broccoli, green beans, spinach)
- Pumpkin seeds, fresh herbs

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the carrots into sticks and toss them with 1 tbsp oil and the cumin. Roast for 25 minutes.
2. Cut the tofu into cubes and mix with the soy sauce and maple syrup. Leave to marinate.
3. Blend the drained beans with the garlic, lemon, 2 tbsp oil and a little water until smooth. Warm the purée gently.
4. Brown the tofu in a pan for 5 to 6 minutes, then sauté the green vegetables for 3 to 4 minutes in the same pan.
5. Plate the purée, carrots, tofu and vegetables. Sprinkle with seeds and herbs.

## Variation
Tofu or egg: replace the tofu with a fried or soft-boiled egg on top of the purée (the recipe is then no longer vegan). The purée keeps for three days in the fridge: make a double batch between D17 and D20.
