To prepare on a day when you feel good, and to put up for the end of your cycle. When everything feels heavy, you no longer have to decide: you follow the list.

Eat simply

  • Ready or quick meals made ahead (freezer, leftovers)
  • A filling snack within reach
  • A hot drink that does me good
  • My guilt-free "comfort" meal: ____________

Move gently

  • A few minutes' walk outside
  • Stretching or gentle yoga in the evening
  • I keep what does me good, I postpone what wears me out

Slow down

  • Earlier bedtime, screens off earlier
  • One thing removed from my diary: ____________
  • A moment just for me: ____________
  • Warmth on my belly if it soothes me

Let people know

  • I told: ____________
  • My sentence to say "I need calm": ____________
  • I don't have to handle everything alone this week

This plan does not replace medical advice. See someone without waiting if:

intense or sudden pain · very heavy bleeding (a pad or tampon soaked in less than an hour) · fever with pelvic pain · feeling faint · possible pregnancy with pain or bleeding · new or worsening symptoms.

Very low mood, overwhelming anxiety: talk to your doctor or a psychologist. Emergency: 15 in France, or your local emergency number.
