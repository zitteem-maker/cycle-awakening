I know that moment: you close a guide and tell yourself "on Monday, I'll change everything". Then the tiredness comes back, the diary overflows, and the guide ends up on a shelf with a bit of guilt on top. So this time, let's do it differently.

Choose **just one thing**. The easiest one, almost too simple. A proper meal at a set time, a walk instead of an intense class, an evening with nothing planned around D26. Do it this cycle. That's enough.

In the first cycle, you observe. In the second, you plan ahead for one or two things. By the third, many habits come naturally. It's not a question of willpower; it's a habit that builds, gently.

The hardest part, I know, isn't knowing what to do. It's remembering at the right moment. That's why I didn't want this guide to be an ending.

And on the days when nothing goes to plan (there will be some), be gentle with yourself. There's no such thing as a failed cycle. There are just cycles that teach you something.
