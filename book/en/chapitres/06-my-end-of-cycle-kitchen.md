At the end of my cycle, I have neither the energy nor the desire to spend an hour in the kitchen. So I keep a few recipes to hand: things that are quick to make, that keep, that fill you up, and that lift your mood.

They all follow the same logic as the "Nourish" pillar: **a complete plate**, flavour, and **no bans**. No calories, no "light version", no guilt.

<aside class="encadre encadre-rose">
<p class="encadre-titre">How to use these recipes</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Between D17 and D20</strong>, make the ones that keep: banana bread, cookies, white bean purée, dal.</li>
<li><strong>Between D21 and D28</strong>, pick from the express recipes: the orange and fromage blanc bowl, the dates, the soy chicken.</li>
<li>Adapt freely: every recipe offers a variation.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

<p class="petit">If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, on treatment or have an allergy, check the ingredients with a health professional. The photos illustrating the recipes are mood photos. Spoon measures: 1 tbsp = 15 ml, 1 tsp = 5 ml.</p>
